01 · Problem Statement
Why We're Changing
The current air-cooled AC setup cools entire floor areas, but only the digital printer heads require precise temperature and humidity control. This creates massive energy waste.

❌ Current Setup — Old Facility

25 AC units across 2 floors
117 Tons total cooling capacity
Entire floor area (~9,200 sq.ft/floor) cooled
Air-cooled split & floor standing units
No standby redundancy for critical zones
Cooling dryer zones, offices, general areas unnecessarily
High energy consumption — 24 hrs/day

✅ Proposed Setup — New Facility

5 enclosed rooms — one per printer head zone only
Water-cooled chiller system — more efficient
Targeted cooling — only Section 2 (Printhead) of each machine
10 FCUs total (2 per room: 1 running + 1 standby)
Full N+1 redundancy — no production downtime risk
2 Chillers × 25 Ton (1 running + 1 standby)
Dramatically lower energy consumption
02 · System Design
New Targeted Cooling Architecture
Each digital printer's Section 2 (Printhead + Carriage zone) gets its own dedicated enclosed room with a dedicated FCU pair. Chillers serve all 5 rooms centrally.
⚙️ Design Philosophy: Digital printheads are sensitive to temperature and humidity fluctuations. Rather than cooling an entire 9,200 sq.ft floor, we contain and control only the ~15–20 sq.ft printhead zone per machine — reducing cooling load by over 80%.

06 · Recommendation
Executive Summary
Recommendation to the CEO for approval of HVAC transition from old facility air-cooled system to targeted chiller-based cooling in new facility.

✅ RECOMMENDED FOR APPROVAL

The proposed transition from a 25-unit, 117-Ton air-cooled floor system to a targeted 5-room, chiller-based printhead cooling architecture delivers significant energy savings, operational reliability through N+1 redundancy, and a clear payback within the projected timeline. This is both a cost-saving and an operational risk-reduction initiative.

01
Energy Efficiency
~83% reduction in active cooling load. Only cool what needs cooling — the printhead zone.
02
Zero Downtime Risk
Full N+1 redundancy on both FCUs and chillers. Standby unit auto-switches if primary fails.
03
Clear ROI
Investment recovers within projected payback period. Every year after is pure saving.
04
Asset Optimization
Existing AC fleet to be redeployed or sold — reducing stranded asset cost.
05
Superior Equipment Lifespan
Standard floor-standing AC units typically last 3–4 years under continuous industrial use. Water-cooled chillers, by contrast, have a proven operational life of 10–15 years — meaning fewer replacements, lower long-term CAPEX, and more stable operations over the facility's lifetime.
